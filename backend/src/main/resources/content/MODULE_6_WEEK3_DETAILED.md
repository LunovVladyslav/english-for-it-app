## ДЕНЬ 155: Structure of a Tutorial

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Beginning, Middle, End
- The Hook

### LESSON 155.1: The Setup (20 хвилин)
1.  **Goal:** What will we build?
2.  **Pre-reqs:** What do you need?
3.  **Steps:** The body.
4.  **Result:** What it looks like.

---

## ДЕНЬ 156: Connecting Steps (Transitions)

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Flow words
- "Next", "Once", "Now"

### LESSON 156.1: Smooth Flow (15 хвилин)
- "Now that we have installed X, let's configure Y."
- "With the server running, we can test the API."

---

## ДЕНЬ 157: Engagement & Tone

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Friendly vs Formal
- Engaging the reader

### LESSON 157.1: Write like you talk (20 хвилин)
- Good: "Let's dive in!"
- Bad: "The user shall proceed to initiate..."

---

## ДЕНЬ 158: Editing & Clarity

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Removing fluff
- Active Voice

### LESSON 158.1: The Knife (20 хвилин)
Cut unnecessary words.
- *Bad:* "It is important to note that you need to save."
- *Good:* "Remember to save."

---

## ДЕНЬ 159: SEO Basics for Devs

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Keywords in Titles
- Meta descriptions

### LESSON 159.1: Being Found (15 хвилин)
- Title: "How to build X with Y" (Actionable).
- Headers: Use H2 for key sections.

---
