## ДЕНЬ 22: Constructive Disagreement

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Навчитися не погоджуватися чемно (Polite Disagreement)
- Використовувати Conditonals для пом'якшення
- Vocabulary: Softening language

### LESSON 22.1: The Art of "No" (20 хвилин)

Disagreement in English IT culture is often indirect. Direct "No" can seem aggressive.

**Direct vs Diplomatic:**
- ❌ "You are wrong." -> ✅ "I see it differently."
- ❌ "That won't work." -> ✅ "I have some concerns about that approach."
- ❌ "We can't do that." -> ✅ "That might be difficult given our timeline."

**The "Yes, And..." Strategy (Validation + Pivot):**
1.  **Validate:** "That's a valid point..." / "I see where you're coming from..."
2.  **Pivot:** "...however, we need to consider..." / "...but from a performance perspective..."

### LESSON 22.2: Grammar - Second Conditional for Diplomacy (20 хвилин)

We use hypothetical structures to make problems sound less "real" and therefore less scary.

**Structure:** `If + Past Simple, ... would + verb`

**Examples:**
- *Direct:* "We don't have budget. We can't buy this tool."
- *Diplomatic:* "If we **had** the budget, we **would** definitely buy it. But right now..."

- *Direct:* "You need to change this code."
- *Diplomatic:* "If you **changed** this structure, it **would** be more readable."

**Practice:** Rewrite.
*   "It is too risky." -> "It **would be** a bit risky."
*   "We don't have time." -> "If we **had** more time..."

## ДЕНЬ 23: Handling Misunderstandings & Clarification

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Розв'язувати непорозуміння (Misunderstandings)
- Фрази для уточнення (Clarification)
- Active Listening in Conflict

### LESSON 23.1: Identifying the Gap (15 хвилин)

Most conflicts are just missed context.

**Phrases to "Stop the Bleeding":**
- "I think we might be talking about different things."
- "Let's take a step back."
- "Correct me if I'm wrong, but..."

### LESSON 23.2: Vocabulary - The "I" Language (15 хвилин)

Avoid "You" statements (Accusatory). Use "I" statements (Experience).

*   ❌ "You didn't explain this well."
*   ✅ "I'm having trouble understanding this part."

*   ❌ "You broke the build."
*   ✅ "I noticed the build is broken."

## ДЕНЬ 24: Depersonalizing Blame (Passive Voice)

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- "Blameless Post-Mortems"
- Grammar: Passive Voice for Diplomacy

### LESSON 24.1: The Blameless Culture (15 хвилин)

In modern IT (DevOps), we focus on the *system*, not the *person*.

**Vocabulary:**
- **Root Cause Analysis (RCA)**: Finding the real issue.
- **Incident**: The bad event.
- **Mitigation**: Fixing it temporarily.

### LESSON 24.2: Grammar - Passive Voice (20 хвилин)

**Use Passive when the "Actor" doesn't matter or you want to be polite.**

*   **Active (Blaming):** "John deleted the table."
*   **Passive (Objective):** "The table **was deleted**."

*   **Active:** "We made a mistake in the config."
*   **Passive:** "A mistake **was made** in the config."

**Practice:**
1.  "Someone pushed bad code." -> "Bad code **was pushed**."
2.  "The team missed the deadline." -> "The deadline **was missed**."

## ДЕНЬ 25: Escalating Issues Professionaly

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Коли і як ескалювати (Escalation)
- Vocabulary: Risks & Impact

### LESSON 25.1: The Escalation Matrix (20 хвилин)

Don't complain. Report risks.

**Bad Escalation:**
"Manager, the API team is ignoring me! Do something!"

**Good Escalation (Facts + Impact + Ask):**
1.  **Facts:** "We are still waiting for the API keys."
2.  **Impact:** "This is blocking the frontend integration work."
3.  **Ask:** "Can you help facilitate a sync with their lead?"

**Vocabulary:**
- **Showstopper**: Critical issue blocking release.
- **Bottlebeck**: Something slowing down the process.
- **Mitigation plan**: Plan B.

## ДЕНЬ 26: Practical Scenarios & Role-Play

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Практика реальних конфліктів
- Assertiveness

### LESSON 26.1: The Pushback (20 хвилин)

**Scenario:** Product Manager pushes for an unrealistic deadline.
**Your Goal:** Say "No" without saying "No".

**Script:**
PM: "We need this by Friday."
You: "I want to help us hit that target. However, looking at the complexity, a Friday release involves high risk of bugs. **What if we** release the core feature on Friday, and the rest next week?" (The "Alternative" technique).
