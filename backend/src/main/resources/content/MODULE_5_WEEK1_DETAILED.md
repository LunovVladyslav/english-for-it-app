## ДЕНЬ 113: Agile Principles & Mindset

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Explaining Agile process
- Active vs Passive Voice in Process Descriptions

### LESSON 113.1: Defining Agile (20 хвилин)
**Key Concept:** Agile is iterative (repeating cycles) vs Waterfall (linear).
**Phrase:** "We work in 2-week sprints to iterate faster."

### LESSON 113.2: Grammar - Passive Voice in Processes (15 хвилин)
- "The backlog **is created** by the Product Owner."
- "Stories **are selected** during planning."
- "Code **is deployed** after testing."

---

## ДЕНЬ 114: The Scrum Team Roles

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Scrum Roles Vocabulary
- Describing Responsibilities

### LESSON 114.1: Who is who? (20 хвилин)
- **Product Owner:** Prioritizes the backlog. (The "What").
- **Scrum Master:** Removes impediments. (The "How").
- **Development Team:** Delivers the increment. (The "Doers").

**Phrase:** "The PO maintains the roadmap, while the SM facilitates the events."

---

## ДЕНЬ 115: Writing User Stories

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- User Story Format
- Acceptance Criteria

### LESSON 115.1: The "As a..." Formula (20 хвилин)
**Standard format:**
"**As a** [user role], **I want** [feature], **so that** [benefit]."
*Example:* "As a shopper, I want to filter by size, so that I can find clothes that fit."

### LESSON 115.2: Acceptance Criteria (AC) (15 хвилин)
- "Verify that..."
- "Ensure that..."
- "Given X, When Y, Then Z" (Gherkin syntax).

---

## ДЕНЬ 116: Sprint Planning & Estimation

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Planning Poker Vocabulary
- Discussing Complexity

### LESSON 116.1: Estimation Talk (20 хвилин)
- "This story is a 5, not a 3, because of the legacy code dependency."
- "Let's **break this down** into smaller tasks."
- "What is our **velocity** for this sprint?"

---

## ДЕНЬ 117: Dailies & Retrospectives

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Standup updates (Recap)
- Retro: "Start, Stop, Continue"

### LESSON 117.1: The Retrospective (20 хвилин)
**Constructive Feedback in Retro:**
- "We should **start** adding unit tests earlier."
- "We need to **stop** merging without reviews."
- "Let's **continue** doing pair programming."

---

## ДЕНЬ 118: Handling Agile Impediments

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Reporting Blockers
- Negotiating Scope

### LESSON 118.1: Raising the Red Flag (20 хвилин)
- "I'm **blocked by** the API unavailability."
- "We might miss the sprint goal **due to** unexpected complexity."
- "**I suggest we move** this story to the next sprint."
