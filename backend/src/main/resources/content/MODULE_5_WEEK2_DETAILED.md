## ДЕНЬ 120: Defining DevOps

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Culture vs Tools
- Causal Connectors (leads to, results in)

### LESSON 120.1: It's not just Jenkins (15 хвилин)
"DevOps is a culture of collaboration that **leads to** faster delivery."

### LESSON 120.2: Grammar - Cause & Effect (20 хвилин)
- "Automated testing **results in** fewer bugs."
- "Lack of monitoring **caused** the outage."
- "**Because of** containers, we ship consistency."
