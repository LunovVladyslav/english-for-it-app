## ДЕНЬ 57: Interrupting Politely

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Як перебивати ввічливо
- Turn-taking signals

### LESSON 57.1: The "Excuse Me" Strategy (20 хвилин)
**Phrases:**
- **Soft:** "May I add something?" / "Quick point here."
- **Hard:** "Sorry to interrupt, but..." (Use sparingly).
- **Checking:** "Can I jump in?"

### LESSON 57.2: Reading Signals (15 хвилин)
Watch for: Falling intonation, Pauses, Eye contact.

---

## ДЕНЬ 58: Active Listening

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Back-channeling (Listening noises)
- Clarifying questions

### LESSON 58.1: Don't Be Silent (15 хвилин)
In English, silence = death/disconnection.
You MUST say: "**Right**", "**Uh-huh**", "**I see**", "**Makes sense**".

### LESSON 58.2: Clarifying (20 хвилин)
- "Could you clarify what you mean by X?"
- "Just to make sure I understand..."
- "Do you mean A or B?"

---

## ДЕНЬ 59: Summarizing & Confirming

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Як робити Summary
- Confirming Understanding

### LESSON 59.1: The Summary (20 хвилин)
"So, if I understand correctly, we are deciding to delay the release?"
"To recap: we will focus on bugs today and features tomorrow."

---

## ДЕНЬ 60: Agreeing & Disagreeing in Meetings

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Expressing Agreement/Disagreement
- Group Dynamics

### LESSON 60.1: Agreeing (15 хвилин)
- "I couldn't agree more."
- "That's a great point."
- "I second that."

### LESSON 60.2: Disagreeing (15 хвилин)
- "I see your point, but..." (The "But" eraser).
- "I'm not so sure about that."
- "I have a concern regarding..."

---

## ДЕНЬ 61: The Daily Standup (Refresher)

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Standup Etiquette Review (recall Module 1)
- Brevity

### LESSON 61.1: Speed Challenge (20 хвилин)
Can you give your update in 45 seconds?
Practice eliminating "filler stories". Focus on Impact.

---

## ДЕНЬ 62: Practice - The Meeting Simulation

### Task: The Interruption Game
Role-play: Talk about a topic. Have the partner (AI) interrupt you. Practice handling it:
- "One second, let me finish."
- "I'll get to that in a moment."

---

## ДЕНЬ 63: Weekly Review

### Review
- Practice your "Interrupting" phrases.
- Check your Active Listening habits (record a call).

### Homework
Watch a YouTube video of a tech panel discussion. Count how many times speakers use "Right", "Yeah", "I see".
