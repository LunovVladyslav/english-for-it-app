## ДЕНЬ 43: Career Goals ("Where do you see yourself?")

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Відповідати на питання про майбутнє
- Grammar: Future Forms

### LESSON 43.1: The Career Map (20 хвилин)
Avoid "I don't know" or "In your chair".
Focus on **Growth** and **Alignment**.
"I plan to deepen my expertise in Cloud Native architecture and eventually lead a small team."

### LESSON 43.2: Grammar - Future Forms (20 хвилин)
- **Plan:** "I **am going to** get AWS certified."
- **Hope:** "I **hope to** work on high-load systems."
- **Definite:** "I **am starting** a course next week."

---

## ДЕНЬ 44: Money Talk (Salary Negotiation)

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Говорити про зарплату (Compensation)
- Vocabulary: Gross/Net, Perks

### LESSON 44.1: Negotiation Vocabulary (15 хвилин)
- **Base Salary**: Fixed amount.
- **Equity/Stock**: Ownership.
- **Sign-on bonus**: One-time payment.
- **Market rate**: Average pay for role.

### LESSON 44.2: Phrasing the Ask (20 хвилин)
"Based on market research and my experience, I'm looking for a range between X and Y."
"I'm flexible, but my priority is the total compensation package."

---

## ДЕНЬ 45: Questions to Ask the Interviewer

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Показати зацікавленість через питання
- Reverse Interviewing

### LESSON 45.1: Strategic Questions (20 хвилин)
Asking good questions makes you look smart.
- **Tech:** "How do you handle technical debt?"
- **Culture:** "How are decisions made about new features?"
- **Growth:** "What does success look like in this role?"

---

## ДЕНЬ 46: Researching the Company

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Підготовка до інтерв'ю ("Why us?")
- Vocabulary: Mission, Values

### LESSON 46.1: The "Why Us" Question (20 хвилин)
Connect your values to theirs.
"I saw your talk on [Topic] and I admire your commitment to Open Source. I want to work in an environment that values community."

---

## ДЕНЬ 47: Leaving Your Current Job

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- "Why are you leaving?"
- Staying Positive

### LESSON 47.1: The Exit Story (15 хвилин)
Never badmouth your current boss.
**Formula:** Pushed by Lack of Growth + Pulled by New Opportunity.
"I've learned a lot at [Current Company], but I've hit a ceiling in terms of technical challenge. I'm looking for a role where I can work with [New Tech]."

---

## ДЕНЬ 48: Practice - Salary Negotiation

### Task: The Offer
Scenario provided by AI: "We can only offer $X (which is lower than you want)."
Your goal: Negotiate for higher pay or more vacation/equity.
**Phrase:** "Is there any flexibility on the base salary? I was expecting something closer to Y."
