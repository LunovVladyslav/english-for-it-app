## ДЕНЬ 79: The Hostile Questioner

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- De-escalation
- Rephrasing

### LESSON 79.1: The R.A.A. Technique (Rephrase, Acknowledge, Answer) (20 хвилин)
Q: "Why is this so buggy?"
A: "You're asking about stability (Rephrase). It's a key priority (Acknowledge). We are patching it today (Answer)."

## ДЕНЬ 81: Impromptu Speaking

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Thinking on your feet
- PREP Structure

### LESSON 81.1: PREP (Point, Reason, Example, Point) (20 хвилин)
Q: "What do you think of AI?"
A: "I think it's useful (P). Because it saves time (R). For example, I use it for regex (E). So, it's a net positive (P)."
