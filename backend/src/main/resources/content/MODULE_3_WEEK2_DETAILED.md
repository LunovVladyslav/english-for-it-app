## ДЕНЬ 64: Opening & Agenda

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Starting a meeting
- Setting Agenda (P.O.P.)

### LESSON 64.1: The Strong Start (20 хвилин)
**P.O.P.** = Purpose, Outcome, Process.
"The **Purpose** is to fix the bug. The **Outcome** is a merged PR. The **Process** is to review logs first."

---

## ДЕНЬ 65: Time Management in Meetings

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Controlling Time
- Parking Lot technique

### LESSON 65.1: The Timekeeper (20 хвилин)
- "We have 5 minutes left."
- "Let's table this for now."
- "Let's take this offline."

---

## ДЕНЬ 66: Encouraging Participation

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Getting people to talk
- Addressing quiet people

### LESSON 66.1: The Inviter (20 хвилин)
- "John, what are your thoughts on this?"
- "Does anyone else have an opinion?"
- "Let's hear from the frontend team."

---

## ДЕНЬ 67: Managing Conflict in Meetings

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Mediator Skills
- Neutralizing arguments

### LESSON 67.1: The Peacemaker (20 хвилин)
"It seems we have two different opinions here. Let's look at the data."
"Let's vote or defer this decision."

---

## ДЕНЬ 68: Closing & Action Items

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Action Items (Who does what)
- Closing

### LESSON 68.1: The Wrap-up (20 хвилин)
"To summarize, John does X, Sarah does Y."
"Action items are..."
"Thanks everyone, great session."

---

## ДЕНЬ 69: Practice - Lead a Standup

### Task: Facilitate
You are the Scrum Master. Start the standup, call on people ("Next is Alex"), handle someone talking too long ("Great detail, let's discuss offline"), and close.

---

## ДЕНЬ 70: Weekly Review

### Review
- Check your P.O.P. structure.
- Practice "Parking Lot" phrases.
