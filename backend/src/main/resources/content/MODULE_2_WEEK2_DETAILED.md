## ДЕНЬ 36: The STAR Method

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Зрозуміти метод STAR
- Vocabulary: Achievement Verbs

### LESSON 36.1: What is STAR? (20 хвилин)

The standard for "Tell me about a time..." questions.

*   **S - Situation**: Context (10%). "We had a legacy monolith..."
*   **T - Task**: Goal (10%). "I needed to extract the payment module..."
*   **A - Action**: Your contribution (60%). "I analyzed dependencies, wrote the API in Go, and migrated data..." (Use "I", not "We").
*   **R - Result**: Outcome (20%). "Deploy times dropped by 50%."

### LESSON 36.2: Achievement Verbs (15 хвилин)
Stop saying "I did". Say:
- **Architected** (Designed).
- **Spearheaded** (Led).
- **Optimized** (Improved).
- **Redesigned** (Changed structure).

## ДЕНЬ 37: Talking About Failures

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Відповідати на "Tell me about a failure"
- Grammar: Past Simple vs Present Perfect

### LESSON 37.1: The "Failure" Strategy (20 хвилин)
Never say "I don't fail".
1.  **Admit:** "I crashed prod."
2.  **Fix:** "I rolled back immediately."
3.  **Learn:** "I added automated tests to prevent recurrence."

### LESSON 37.2: Grammar - Used to / Would (20 хвилин)
Talking about past habits.
- "I **used to** code in PHP." (Not anymore).
- "We **would** deploy on Fridays." (Repeated past action).

---

## ДЕНЬ 38: Handling Pressure & Conflict

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Conflict Stories
- Grammar: Result Clauses

### LESSON 38.1: The Conflict Story (20 хвилин)
Focus on **Resolution**, not Drama.
"My peer wanted SQL, I wanted NoSQL. We compared benchmarks. SQL was safer. I agreed to go with his choice."

### LESSON 38.2: Grammar - Result Clauses (15 хвилин)
- **So** (Casual): "It broke, **so** I fixed it."
- **Therefore** (Formal): "It broke; **therefore**, I initiated a rollback."
- **Consequently**: "The API changed; **consequently**, the frontend broke."

---

## ДЕНЬ 39: Describing Your Strengths & Weaknesses

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- "Greatest Strength/Weakness"
- Adjectives for Personality

### LESSON 39.1: The Weakness Trap (20 хвилин)
Don't say "I work too hard" (Fake) or "I'm lazy" (Real but bad).
Say a **Real Weakness + Fix**.
"I sometimes get lost in details (**Weakness**). So now I set a timer to check the big picture every hour (**Fix**)."

### LESSON 39.2: Vocabulary - Strengths (15 хвилин)
- **Adaptable**: Fast learner.
- **Resilient**: Don't give up.
- **Team-oriented**: Collaborative.
- **Detail-oriented**: Careful.

## ДЕНЬ 40: Remote Work & Teamwork Questions

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Розповідати про стиль роботи
- Vocabulary: Async, Sync, Remote culture

### LESSON 40.1: Describing Workflow (20 хвилин)
Q: "How do you handle remote work?"
A: "I over-communicate. I use Slack for updates, document everything in Confluence, and prefer async discussions to minimize meetings."

Vocabulary:
- **Over-communicate**: Communicate more than necessary to ensure clarity.
- **Time zones**: Handling overlap.
- **Deep work**: Interrupt-free coding time.
