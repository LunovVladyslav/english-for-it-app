## ДЕНЬ 85: Professional Email Structure

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Структура імейлу (Subject, Body, Closing)
- Register: Formal vs Informal

### LESSON 85.1: The 4-Part Structure (20 хвилин)
1.  **Subject Line:** Actionable. "Urgent" is not enough. Better: "Action Required: API Key Expiration".
2.  **Salutation:** "Hi [Name]" (Standard), "Dear [Name]" (Formal).
3.  **Body (BLUF - Bottom Line Up Front):** State purpose immediately.
4.  **Closing:** "Best regards", "Thanks".

### LESSON 85.2: Register (15 хвилин)
- **Formal:** "I am writing to inquire..."
- **Neutral:** "I'm writing to ask..."
- **Informal:** "Checking in on..."

## ДЕНЬ 86: Requests & Politeness

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Making Requests
- Softening language

### LESSON 86.1: The Power of "Could" (20 хвилин)
Don't say "Send me the file."
- "**Could you please** send the file?"
- "**Would it be possible to** get access?"
- "I **was wondering if** you could help."

---

## ДЕНЬ 87: Status Updates via Email/Slack

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Updates and Delays
- Conciseness

### LESSON 87.1: The Status Check (20 хвилин)
- "**Just a quick update on** the migration..."
- "**We are on track to** release by Friday."
- "**There has been a slight delay due to** X."

---

## ДЕНЬ 88: Follow-ups & Reminders

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Chasing people politely
- "Per my last email" alternates

### LESSON 88.1: Nudging (20 хвилин)
- "**Just bubbling this up to the top of your inbox.**"
- "**Checking in to see if** you had a chance to review."
- "**Following up on** my previous message."

---

## ДЕНЬ 89: Professional Slack Etiquette

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Chat vs Email
- Threading

### LESSON 89.1: Rules of Chat (15 хвилин)
1.  **Don't say "Hello" and wait.** Say "Hello, I need help with X" (No-Hello rule).
2.  **Use Threads.** Don't clutter the main channel.
3.  **Edit, don't double-post.**
