## ДЕНЬ 106: The Elevator Pitch

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- 60-second pitch
- Hooking the listener

### LESSON 106.1: Structure (20 хвилин)
**Hook -> Problem -> Solution -> Ask.**
"Have you ever waited 10 seconds for a page to load? (Hook). It kills satisfying. (Problem). Our new caching layer fixes this. (Solution). I need approval to deploy. (Ask)."

---

## ДЕНЬ 107: Quantifying Value (ROI)

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Business Vocabulary
- Numbers

### LESSON 107.1: Talking Numbers (20 хвилин)
- "**Reduce** latency by 50%."
- "**Increase** conversion by 10%."
- "**Save** 20 developer hours per week."

---

## ДЕНЬ 108: Handling Objections in a Pitch

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- "Yes, but..."
- Risk mitigation

### LESSON 108.1: Defense (20 хвилин)
- Objection: "It's too risky."
- Response: "We have a rollback plan and will do a canary release."

---

## ДЕНЬ 109: Storytelling in Tech

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Making it personal
- The Hero's Journey (User's Journey)

### LESSON 109.1: The User Logic (20 хвилин)
"Meet Sarah. She tries to login. She fails. She gets angry. With our new feature, she logs in instantly."

## ДЕНЬ 110: Practice - The Shark Tank

### Task: Pitch a Refactor
You need 2 weeks to refactor tech debt. PM says "No features?". Pitch it.
"If we don't do this, development speed will slow by 40% next quarter."
