## ДЕНЬ 99: The Language of Negotiation

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Making proposals
- Conditional Offers

### LESSON 99.1: If... Then... (20 хвилин)
- "**If** you can extend the deadline, **then** we can include that feature."
- "We can offer X, **provided that** you agree to Y."

---

## ДЕНЬ 100: Rejection & Compromise

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Saying No politely
- Meeting halfway

### LESSON 100.1: The Soft No (20 хвилин)
- "I'm afraid that won't work for us."
- "That's a bit out of our budget."
- "We really need to stick to the original scope."

### LESSON 100.2: Compromise (15 хвилин)
- "Could we meet halfway?"
- "How about we trade X for Y?"

---

## ДЕНЬ 101: Stakeholder Management

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Knowing your audience
- Addressing concerns

### LESSON 101.1: Mapping (20 хвилин)
- **Engineers:** Care about technical debt, stability.
- **PMs:** Care about deadline, features.
- **Execs:** Care about ROI, speed.

---

## ДЕНЬ 102: Persuasion Techniques

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Logical arguments
- Social proof

### LESSON 102.1: Structuring Arguments (20 хвилин)
- "**Not only** is it faster, **but** it is **also** cheaper."
- "Industry standard is moving towards X."

---

## ДЕНЬ 103: Negotiating Timelines

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Pushback on deadlines
- Scope trading

### LESSON 103.1: The Iron Triangle (20 хвилин)
Fast, Good, Cheap. Pick two.
"We can deliver by Friday, but we'll have to cut the testing phase."

---

## ДЕНЬ 104: Practice - The Salary Negotiation 2.0

### Task: The Raise
You want a 10% raise. Your boss says budget is tight.
**Script:** "I understand the constraints. However, considering my contribution to Project X, which saved $50k, I believe an adjustment is warranted."
