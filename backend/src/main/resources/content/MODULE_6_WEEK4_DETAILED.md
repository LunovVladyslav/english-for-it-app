# ТИЖДЕНЬ 4: PROFESSIONAL REPORTS
## Days 162-168: Детальний розклад

---

## ДЕНЬ 162: Incident Reports (Post-Mortems)

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Factual Tone
- No Blame Language

### LESSON 162.1: Just the Facts (20 хвилин)
- **Summary:** What happened?
- **Impact:** Who was affected?
- **Resolution:** How was it fixed?

---

## ДЕНЬ 163: Status Reports

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Highlights & Lowlights
- Conciseness

### LESSON 163.1: Weekly Email (20 хвилин)
- **Done:** Completed items.
- **In Progress:** Current work.
- **Blockers:** Help needed.

---

## ДЕНЬ 164: Proposals & RFCs

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Persuasive Writing (Written Pitch)
- Problem/Solution

### LESSON 164.1: Request for Comments (20 хвилин)
- "I propose we use X because..."
- "Alternatives considered: Y and Z."

---

## ДЕНЬ 165: Final Editing Checklist

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Proofreading
- Consistency

### LESSON 165.1: The Polish (20 хвилин)
- Check spelling (Typos destroy trust).
- Check formatting consistency.

---

## ДЕНЬ 166: MODULE 6 REVIEW

### Review Topics
- Imperatives (Instruction).
- API Terms.
- Tutorial Structure.
