# ТИЖДЕНЬ 3: PRESENTATION SKILLS
## Days 71-77: Детальний розклад

---

## ДЕНЬ 71: Sequencing & Signposting

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Structuring a talk
- Transitions

### LESSON 71.1: The Roadmap (20 хвилин)
- **Start**: "I'd like to begin with..."
- **Move**: "Moving on to..."
- **End**: "To conclude..."

---

## ДЕНЬ 72: Visuals & Describing Data

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Explaining Charts/Screenshots
- Vocabulary: Increase/Decrease

### LESSON 72.1: Chart Talk (20 хвилин)
- "As you can see..."
- "This graph illustrates..."
- "Notice the spike here."

---

## ДЕНЬ 73: Engagement & Hook

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Grabbing attention
- Storytelling

### LESSON 73.1: The Hook (20 хвилин)
Start with a question, a stat, or a story.
"Did you know 40% of users leave if load time is slow?"

---

## ДЕНЬ 74: Body Language & Voice

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Intonation
- Online presence

### LESSON 74.1: The Camera (15 хвилин)
Look at the camera, not the screen. Smile. Use gestures.

---

## ДЕНЬ 75: Demo Day Skills

### Навчальні цілі дня:
- Knowing your Demo flow
- Handling tech failures

### LESSON 75.1: The Demo Murphy's Law (20 хвилин)
If it can break, it will.
"It seems the demo gods are not with us today. Here is a screenshot of what it should look like."

---

## ДЕНЬ 76: Practice - The 5 Minute Demo

### Task: Prepare & Record
Prepare a 5-minute demo of a feature (real or fake).
Use: Hook -> Signposting -> Visual explanation -> Summary.

---

## ДЕНЬ 77: Weekly Review

### Review
- Check your transition phrases.
- Review "Tech Failure" recovery phrases.
